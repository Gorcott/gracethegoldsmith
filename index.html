What We Do

Grace the Goldsmith
services

Every piece begins with a conversation. Whether you arrive with a clear vision, a rough sketch, or a handful of inherited gold, Grace will guide you from first idea to finished jewel.

01

Bespoke Design

Your vision, realised from scratch. Engagement rings, pendants, earrings — each piece is designed and crafted entirely for you, with no two ever the same.

02

Manufacturing

From casting and forging to hand-setting stones, every stage of manufacture is completed by Grace in her Birmingham studio to the highest artisanal standard.

03

Repurposing

Give old gold and precious stones a new life. Inherited or unworn jewellery is transformed into contemporary pieces that carry forward their history and meaning.

04

Repair & Restoration

Broken clasps, worn settings, damaged bands — Grace restores treasured pieces with the same care and precision she brings to creating new ones.
